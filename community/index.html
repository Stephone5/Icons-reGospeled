Come to the table

Faith is better with a table and people around it.

Our gatherings are marked by grace, attentiveness, and real human connection — places to bring your questions, wounds, hopes, and stories, and be known without being shamed.

🍗

Wings, Whiskey & the Word

A bi-weekly gathering built on shared meals and meaningful conversation about theology, psychology, and the real stories we carry. More than a casual hangout — an invitation to belong, to be known, and to share in fellowship that's grounded and deeply human.

BYOA (bring your own alcohol). The ministry does not provide or serve alcohol. Participants sign a release/waiver and agree to Christlike conduct. No underage drinking, ever.

🎬

Movies for Humans: Cinema for the Common Good

A monthly gathering that uses film and conversation to cultivate Christlike awareness, honesty, and deeper connection. A thoughtful movie, a meal, and real discussion — an invitation to pay attention together and become more awake, compassionate, and human in one another's presence.

🏕️

Men's Retreat

A gathering designed to help men slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with God, themselves, and one another. Set apart from the noise of everyday life, it makes room for worship, honest conversation, rest, and real brotherhood.

🥃

Kentucky Gentlemen's Retreat — Theology. Worship. Whiskey.

Not our own event, but so aligned with our mission that we go every year — always a highlight. Trinitarian and Christological, with a heavy emphasis on fellowship. Featuring Dr. C. Baxter Kruger (Torrance scholar) and host John Crowder. Oct 1–3, 2026 · Bardstown, KY.
