More informed & dignified theology and psychology

What if the Good News is better than you were told?

Many of us were handed a God who keeps score. Icons reGospeled helps the wounded and the curious rediscover a gospel that heals — through honest theology, psychology, and real community.

Find your book → or take the course

If this is you

You love Christ, but the loudest version of faith started to feel like a threat.

You went looking for something both theologically serious and non-shaming, and couldn't find it. Underneath is the exhaustion, the disillusionment, and the quiet fear that maybe the problem is you.

It isn't. Fear-based, shame-based religion has always struggled to tell the truth about a God who is, first and last, love. The gospel was supposed to be good news — a Physician, not a scorekeeper.

You're not crazy for wanting more

We've been the wounded and disillusioned too.

Icons reGospeled sits at the intersection of ancient theology, modern psychology, and relational presence. We read the Church Fathers and take the brain and the body seriously, because healing is a whole-person thing.

There's a podcast, a course, a table you can actually pull up a chair to, and a curated library of the best restoration theology we know.

What's at stake

You can stop managing your fear and start becoming a living icon again.

The alternative is staying trapped in a faith that slowly crushes you — or walking away from Christ entirely because the only version you knew was toxic. There's another road: hearing the gospel as truly good news, the healing of the whole person, and a community that thinks deeply and loves well.
